Notes

App alias switched: “VestingWrapper” now points to the upgradeable proxy 0x083D…, and legacy 0xbf79… is marked deprecated in deployments/base.json.
The DAO proposal helper is available to update the wrapper’s dependencies:
npx hardhat run scripts/propose-wrapper-setter.js --network base -- action=verify new=0xNEW_VERIFY
actions: verify | metrics | content; optional: wrapper=0xWRAPPER_PROXY, desc="…"
